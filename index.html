Symposium Verticals

Engineered for Physical Makers

Unlike standard software events, TECHNOVA focuses squarely on physical silicon, embedded electronics, autonomous robotics, and edge deployment.

VLSI & Circuit Design

Schematic capture, 4-layer PCB routing, impedance matching, and digital logic synthesis on modern EDA tools.

Robotics & Avionics

Combat bot arenas, line-maze navigators, custom flight controllers, and obstacle-avoidance micro quadcopters.

Edge AI & TinyML

Squeezing neural network models directly onto microcontrollers for real-time sensor analytics without cloud roundtrips.

Physical Prototyping

Access to 3D printers, laser cutters, Hakko soldering stations, and Rigol 4-channel oscilloscopes in our open maker spaces.
